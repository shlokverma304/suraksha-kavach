01 · Introduction

Making Every Drive Safer

Driver drowsiness and blind-spot hazards are two common risks on the road. Our project combines driver monitoring, proximity detection and emergency communication into one compact ESP32-based safety system.

Core idea: Detect the risk → warn the driver → verify response → escalate if necessary.
02 · Real-Life Problem

Small Delays Can Become Big Risks

😴

Driver Drowsiness

Prolonged eye closure can reduce driver awareness and reaction time.

🚗

Blind-Spot Objects

Nearby vehicles or objects may remain outside the driver's direct field of view.

⚠️

No Driver Response

A warning is not enough if the driver remains unresponsive after an alert.

03 · Our Solution

One System. Multiple Layers of Protection.

The ESP32 collects sensor data, identifies risk conditions and activates appropriate responses. Local alerts are used first, while Telegram is used for emergency escalation.

Driver Monitoring

IR eye-blink sensor monitors the driver's eye state and identifies prolonged closure.

Blind-Spot Monitoring

An ultrasonic sensor measures the distance of nearby objects in the monitored blind-spot region.

Local Warning

Buzzer and vibration motor provide immediate driver feedback.

Emergency Escalation

If the driver does not respond, the system can enter emergency mode and send Telegram alerts.

07 · Smart Features

Designed Around Driver Response

⏱️ Timed Drowsiness Detection

Continuous eye closure is evaluated instead of treating every short blink as drowsiness.

📏 Distance-Based Warning

Nearby objects can be categorized into safe, warning and danger ranges.

🔔 Multi-Modal Alert

Combines audible and tactile feedback for a stronger local warning.

🚨 Response-Aware Escalation

Emergency communication is triggered when a detected risk is not resolved.

☁️ Cloud Monitoring

System status and sensor information can be visualized through the dashboard.

📲 Remote Notification

Telegram provides a communication channel for emergency alerts.
